1傾き — liquid, not twitchy

The current tilt tracks the sensor almost 1:1 and snaps, so it feels twitchy and glassy. Refined: heavier inertia (the surface lags, then slosh-overshoots and settles like real liquid), a smaller cap (~7°), and a slow idle sway so it always looks wet. No physics engine, just damping + a settle spring.
Tilt your phone left/right, or drag across the glass on desktop.
