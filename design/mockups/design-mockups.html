What to react to: the lantern-red-on-night palette, the Dela Gothic One display weight, the big rounded game tiles, the playing-card faces, and the persistent safety framing (飲む or 罰ゲーム, base-of-screen notice). The one to scrutinize: the 匿名アンケート pass-around ritual (⑨–⑪) — SPEC §8 says this must be flawless. Easy to change: palette temperature (cooler/neon vs warm izakaya), display face (heavier 祭 energy vs cleaner), tile density, and whether the mood is rowdy-loud or friendly-warm. Tell me which direction to push and I'll revise before we restyle the real UI.
